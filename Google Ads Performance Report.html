        An action is conversions plus phone calls; cost per action is spend divided by that sum. If a call is also counted as a conversion in Google Ads, it counts twice. Active days are the weekdays Oct 5–9 and Oct 12–16; no ads ran on Oct 10, 11, 17 or 18. Hour data covers all campaigns together; calls by hour only count hours that had impressions. Keyword data lists keywords with $25 or more of spend and search terms with $35 or more, so the tables are the expensive end, not every row. Keyword and search-term reports only record conversions. Campaign totals add up the daily rows. Paused campaigns (the three American Income ones, Performance Max and Colonial Penn) are included for the days they ran. Google keeps revising recent numbers.
